# Real-Estate-Dashboard

Project Overview

The Real Estate Dashboard is a business intelligence project developed to analyze property market trends, sales performance, pricing patterns, and location-based insights. The dashboard helps stakeholders make informed investment and business decisions through interactive visualizations and key performance indicators.

Objectives
Analyze property sales and market trends.
Monitor property prices across locations.
Track sales performance and revenue.
Identify high-demand areas and property types.
Provide actionable insights for real estate decision-making.
Tools & Technologies
Power BI
SQL
Microsoft Excel
Data Cleaning & Transformation
Data Visualization
Key Features
Interactive dashboard with filters and slicers.
Property sales and revenue analysis.
Location-wise property performance tracking.
Property type distribution analysis.
Price trend and market trend visualization.
KPI cards for total sales, revenue, average property price, and transactions.
Dynamic reports for business insights.
Key Insights
Identified top-performing locations based on sales volume.
Analyzed property price trends across different regions.
Compared performance of various property types.
Tracked monthly and yearly sales growth patterns.

Business Impact

The dashboard enables real estate professionals, investors, and management teams to evaluate market opportunities, optimize pricing strategies, and make data-driven investment decisions.
